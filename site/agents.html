What we look for

Who makes a great agent

  • A fixed location with regular foot traffic (shop, kiosk, market stall)
  • Valid ID for agent KYC (BVN/NIN)
  • Reliable cash float to serve deposits and withdrawals
  • A phone that runs the CirclePay agent app
  • Trust in your community, which you already have
