We didn't invent saving together. We made it safe.

Across Nigeria and much of Africa, people have always pooled money in trusted circles: Ajo, Esusu, Adashi, Susu, Chama. It works because of community. It breaks because it's manual. CirclePay AI keeps the first and fixes the second.

The problem isn't trust. It's tooling.

A thrift collector with a notebook holds real trust, but a notebook can be lost, fudged or stolen. Friends raise burial money over WhatsApp with the best intentions, but nobody can see the total, the top givers or where the cash went. Rent, school fees and hospital bills arrive as one big number when most people are paid in small ones. And banks, scoring individual credit history, simply can't see the years of reliability someone has built inside their circle.

The result: millions of hard-working people are trusted by their neighbours and invisible to the financial system. CirclePay AI exists to close that gap — to give community finance the rails, transparency and protection it has always deserved, without asking anyone to abandon the culture that makes it work.

The operating system for community finance — where people save together, support each other, pay for life events, and grow businesses, in one place.

What we hold to

The principles behind every screen

Trust is the product

Every feature reduces a specific fear: default, fraud, hidden fees, losing cash. Escrow, a backup pool and clear numbers, not slogans.

Rooted, not imported

We build for how communities already save. The circle is our core, Naira is our language, and the culture leads the design.

Nobody left offline

Agents, kiosks and scratch cards are first-class, not a footnote. If you have no smartphone or bank account, you still belong.

Honesty as tone

Fees, risk and “coming soon” are stated plainly. Candour is how a money brand earns belief, and keeps it.

Where we're going

A roadmap built in phases

Phase 1

The savings loop

Circles, PartPay, CircleSupport, the agent network, wallet and Trust Score — a launchable community-finance app.

Phase 2

Recurring rails

Landlord and school collections, auto-debit mandates, and group governance rules for larger circles.

Phase 3

Broader payments

Buy-now-pay-later, vendor integrations and emergency funds that widen what a circle can do.

Phase 4

Business suite

SME point-of-sale, inventory and working-capital lending, built on the trust members already earned.

Phase 5

Scale & new verticals

A nationwide agent footprint, micro-insurance and diaspora remittances across emerging markets.
