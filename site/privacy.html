Overview

CirclePay AI (“CirclePay”, “we”, “us”) provides a community-finance platform for savings circles, installment payments, fundraising and agent banking. This policy explains what personal and financial information we handle, why, and the choices you have. By using CirclePay you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Identity & KYC: name, phone number, date of birth, BVN/NIN and ID documents, as required to verify you and meet regulatory obligations.
  • Financial: linked account or card details, wallet balances, contributions, payouts, and transaction history.
  • Usage: device information, app interactions and log data used to keep the service secure and reliable.
  • Location: approximate location, only when you use the agent locator.

How we use it

We use your information to operate circles, process contributions and payouts, run PartPay and CircleSupport, power the agent network, and calculate your Trust Score. We also use it to detect fraud and predict default risk, to meet legal and regulatory requirements, and to support and communicate with you. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the service: with payment processors and banks to move money, with identity-verification providers for KYC, with your circle members (limited to what a circle needs, such as paid/unpaid status), and with regulators or authorities where the law requires it. Partners are bound to protect your data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Payments require a PIN or biometric authorisation. Withdrawal codes and scratch-card serials are single-use and time-limited. We maintain audit logs and monitor for fraud. No system is perfectly secure, but protecting a community's money is the core of how we build.

Your rights

You can access and update your information in the app, request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete data we are not legally required to keep. You can manage notification preferences at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as required to meet legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations (including anti-fraud and audit requirements). When no longer needed, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@circlepay.ai. We will respond within a reasonable time. See also our Terms of Service.
