Rotating savings

Circles

Digitise Ajo, Esusu, Adashi and Susu. Members contribute a fixed amount on a set schedule into an escrowed group wallet, and each cycle one member automatically receives the full pool, until everyone has been paid.

  • Set amount, frequency, member count and start date
  • AI-optimised or custom payout order
  • Invite by link; members accept the circle rules
  • Live dashboard: paid / pending / late, group balance, countdown
  • Backup pool reserves a slice to cover defaults

Installments

PartPay

Split rent, school fees, medical bills or a new phone into installments you can afford. Choose the model that fits your cash flow, with every fee shown before you agree.

  • Pay gradually: funds held in escrow, released to the vendor when complete
  • Pay upfront: CirclePay settles the vendor now, you repay over time
  • Transparent interest and schedule, shown up front
  • Upfront model gated by a Trust Score of 700+

Cash & offline

Agent & Kiosk Network

Every user gets a CirclePay ID that works like an account number. Deposit and withdraw cash at local agents, top up with scratch cards, and cash out with a secure one-time code. No smartphone or bank account required.

  • Buy a scratch card, fund your wallet instantly
  • Find nearby agents with distance and open/closed status
  • Time-limited, single-use withdrawal codes
  • Fraud controls: serial validation, agent KYC, single-use codes
