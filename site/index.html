The core loop

One circle, everyone paid, in turn

Members contribute a fixed amount at an agreed interval into a shared, escrowed pool. Each cycle, one member automatically receives the full lump sum, until everyone has been paid. No collector, no chasing, no “where's my money.”

A real cycle

10 members contribute ₦10,000 weekly.

Every week, one member automatically collects ₦100,000, until all ten have received a payout.

  • Automated deductionsLink your bank or card once. Contributions debit on schedule, with no manual chasing.
  • Smart escrow & backup poolA slice of every contribution is reserved. If someone defaults, the pool protects the scheduled payout.
  • AI trust & alertsThe AI watches payment behaviour, warns the group early when someone is at risk, and nudges before payout day.

Good questions

Before you trust us with your savings

What happens if a member doesn't pay?

The AI flags at-risk members early and alerts the group before payout day. If a default still happens, the backup pool (a reserved slice of each cycle) can cover it so the scheduled receiver still gets their full lump sum. Retry logic and a grace period run before anyone is flagged.

Do I need a bank account or smartphone?

No. Every user gets a CirclePay ID that works like an account number. You can deposit and withdraw cash at any agent or kiosk, save with scratch cards, and cash out with a one-time code. No smartphone or bank account required.

How does CirclePay make money?

Transparent, stated fees: small contribution and collection service fees, optional interest on “pay upfront” installments, a 1–3% fundraising platform fee, and agent commissions. Every fee is shown before you confirm, never hidden.

Is my money actually safe?

Funds pool into escrowed, auditable group wallets with a consistent ledger. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, transactions need PIN or Face ID, and the platform is built for CBN alignment, licensed agent operations and full audit logs.

What is the Trust Score, and how do I raise it?

It's an AI reputation built from your on-time contributions, circle participation and transaction patterns. Show up for your circle and it climbs, unlocking micro-loans, early payouts and “pay upfront” installment approvals.
