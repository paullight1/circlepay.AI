Circles & safety

What is a savings circle?

A group of members who contribute a fixed amount on a set schedule into a shared, escrowed pool. Each cycle, one member automatically receives the full lump sum, until everyone has been paid. It digitises Ajo, Esusu, Adashi and Susu.

What happens if a member doesn't pay?

The app retries the debit and allows a grace period before flagging a default, and the AI warns the group early if someone looks at risk. If a default still happens, the backup pool (a reserved slice of each cycle) can cover it so the scheduled receiver gets their full amount.

Who decides the payout order?

The circle creator sets it, either manually or AI-optimised by Trust Score. Everyone sees and agrees to the order and rules before joining.

Is my money held safely?

Funds pool into an escrowed, auditable group wallet with a consistent ledger. Money only moves on schedule, to the designated member. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and every payment needs PIN or Face ID.

Payments, fees & PartPay

How does CirclePay make money?

Transparent, stated fees: small contribution and collection service fees, optional interest on the PartPay “pay upfront” model, a 1–3% fundraising platform fee, and agent commissions. Every fee is shown before you confirm.

What is the difference between the two PartPay models?

Pay gradually: your money is held in escrow until the total is reached, then released to the vendor. Pay upfront: CirclePay settles the vendor now and you repay over time, optionally with small interest. The upfront model requires a Trust Score of 700+.

Can I pay rent or school fees in installments?

Yes. PartPay is built for exactly this: rent, school fees, medical bills and consumer products, split into installments you can afford, with the schedule and fees shown up front.

Accounts, agents & the offline network

Do I need a bank account or smartphone?

No. Every user gets a CirclePay ID that works like an account number. You can deposit and withdraw cash at any agent or kiosk, save with scratch cards, and cash out with a one-time code. No smartphone or bank account required.

How do scratch cards work?

Buy a CirclePay savings card from any agent, scratch it, and enter the serial number in the app. The value is added to your wallet instantly. Cards come in ₦1,000, ₦5,000 and ₦10,000.

How do I withdraw cash at a kiosk?

Request a withdrawal in the app, which generates a time-limited, single-use code. Show it to any CirclePay agent, who verifies your ID and hands you the cash. The fee and total deduction are shown before you confirm. Never share the code with anyone but the agent.

What KYC do I need?

Sign-up is phone-first with OTP. Tiered KYC (BVN/NIN and an ID upload) unlocks higher limits, payouts and installments.

Trust Score

What is the Trust Score and how do I raise it?

It's an AI reputation built from your on-time contributions, circle participation and transaction patterns. Show up for your circle and it climbs, unlocking micro-loans, early payouts and “pay upfront” installment approvals.

Does a missed payment hurt my score?

It can, but a single failed debit that's retried and settled within the grace period is treated differently from a genuine default. Consistency over time is what matters most.
