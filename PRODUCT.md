# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Everyday Nigerians (expanding to Africa and emerging markets) who already trust
community finance — the rotating savings culture of Ajo, Esusu, Adashi, Susu and
Chama — but are underserved by banks that only score individual credit history.

- **Circle savers** — salary earners and traders in savings groups who want
  automated, transparent contributions and payouts.
- **Unbanked / offline users** — no smartphone or bank account; rely on agents,
  kiosks and scratch cards to save and cash out.
- **Campaign organizers** — raising funds for life events (burials, medical,
  weddings, school fees) who need transparent, shareable fundraising.
- **Micro-business owners** and **agent/kiosk operators** — later-phase, but part
  of the vision.

Context of use: mostly mobile, often on constrained bandwidth, deciding whether to
trust a money app with their savings and their community's money. The landing page
must earn that trust before the first tap.

## Product Purpose

CirclePay AI is the **operating system for community finance** — it digitises
traditional rotating savings and blends it with modern fintech rails (open banking,
an agent/kiosk network, an AI trust-and-risk layer). People save together in trusted
circles, split big bills into installments (PartPay), raise funds for life events
(CircleSupport), and reach cash through agents and scratch cards — with escrow, a
backup pool and AI default-prediction protecting every payout.

Success for this landing page: a visitor understands the four products, believes
their money and their community's money are safe, and joins the launch waitlist /
intends to download when live.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy · Warm · Community-rooted. The voice is confident but human — it speaks
the language of Ajo/Esusu, uses real Naira figures, and never hides fees or risk.
Modern fintech precision in service of an old, communal idea: we already know how to
save together; CirclePay just makes it safe, transparent and automatic.

Emotional goals: **safety** (my money is protected), **belonging** (this is for my
community, in my context), and **quiet confidence** (this is real, regulated, and
built with care) — not hype, not Silicon-Valley coldness.

## Anti-references

- Generic purple-gradient SaaS landing pages with floating phone mockups,
  glassmorphism, and a hero-metric stat template — the fintech default.
- Navy-and-gold "serious bank" cliché, or cold Western neobank minimalism that
  erases the community/African context.
- Crypto/"get rich" hype energy, aggressive urgency, or anything that reads as a
  scam — the exact opposite of the trust this category requires.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display-serif + italic + ruled columns) — wrong
  register for a consumer money app.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is the product.** Every section should reduce a specific fear (defaults,
   fraud, hidden fees, losing cash). Show escrow, backup pool, KYC and real numbers.
2. **Rooted, not generic.** The design carries the Ajo/Esusu culture — the "circle"
   is the signature visual metaphor; Naira and real local scenarios ground it.
3. **Show the real product.** Faithful app mockups and true data-viz (the trust
   gauge, the rotating circle) over abstract marketing fluff.
4. **Include the offline majority.** The agent/kiosk/scratch-card story is
   first-class, not a footnote — inclusion is part of the brand.
5. **Honesty as tone.** Fees, risk and "coming soon" states are stated plainly;
   candor is how a money brand earns belief.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1; visible focus states;
  semantic landmarks and headings; all interactive controls keyboard-operable.
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support — the signature circle animation and all
  reveals degrade to static/instant.
- Low-bandwidth conscious: self-contained assets, no heavy media, SVG over raster.
- Never rely on color alone for status (pair with icon/label), given color-vision
  and low-quality-screen users.
